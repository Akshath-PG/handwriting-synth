✍️ Handwriting Synth

Turn any document into your own handwriting. Everything stays on your computer.

1

Print the template and write on it

This sheet has a small box for every letter, number and symbol. You write each one once, in your normal handwriting.

  • Print it on normal A4 paper (4 pages).
  • Use a dark pen. Write one character per box, sitting on the solid line.
  • Keep the writing inside the box. Do not touch the four black squares.
⬇ Download the template
2

Take photos of your pages and upload them

Photograph each page flat on a table, in even light, with all 4 black squares visible. Choose all 4 photos together, then make sure they are in page order (1, 2, 3, 4).

3

Choose a document

Pick a PDF, a Word file (.docx) or a text file. You get it back in your handwriting.
