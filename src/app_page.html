✍️ Handwriting Synth

Turn any document into your own handwriting. Everything stays on your computer.

1

Choose your document

The PDF, Word file (.docx) or text file you want written by hand. It is studied first, so your template only asks for what this document needs. For a Markdown file with pictures, choose the pictures together with it.

2

Choose your handwriting style

How do you normally write? Pick the one closest to your own hand.

3

Print the template and write on it

  • Print it on normal A4 paper. Use a dark pen.
  • Letters: one per box, sitting on the solid line. In cursive, write each letter with its joining strokes, from the left dot to the right dot.
  • Words: write each word in one go, exactly as you would in a sentence.
  • Sentences: write them naturally on the line, at your normal speed, with normal spaces between words. Every word you write there is reused, and your spacing, slant and joins are measured from them.
  • Equations: write them the way you normally would by hand; they are used exactly as you wrote them.
  • Figures: trace over the grey drawing (or draw your own). Leave a box empty and the app sketches that figure for you.
  • Keep writing inside the boxes. Do not touch the black squares or the small squares at the bottom.

4

Take photos of your pages and upload them

Photograph each page flat on a table, in even light, with all 4 black squares visible. Choose all the photos together; the order does not matter.

5

Make it handwritten

Human touch: lines that are not perfectly level, a wandering margin, words that lean and sit slightly differently, uneven ink. Small enough to stay neat, enough that it does not look printed.

Want one template for every document? Download the universal template (174 characters, print style), then upload its 4 photos in step 4.
